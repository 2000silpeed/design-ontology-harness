## Preserve

- Editorial sports-data character rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.
- Thin structural rules, calm light surfaces, compact controls, and restrained depth.
- Strong asymmetric desktop composition with one persistent contextual rail.
- Tabular time and score typography, readable Korean UI copy, and nearby context notes.
- A single decisive selection state that visually connects the primary workspace and rail.

## Product-system overrides

The image is a visual direction, not a literal implementation blueprint. The accepted product concept and component contracts override these image details:

- Replace the decorative orbit/bracket visualization with the fixture scan surface and schedule table.
- Put date, group, team, and status filters before the first fixture row.
- Synchronize the selected fixture, match detail, prediction, result context, discussion, and composer with `selectedMatchId`.
- Keep official, sample, fan, and model data modes and verification states adjacent to their data.
- Use the generated `--ds-*` Semantic OS roles from `tokens.css`; do not sample or copy colors from the reference image.
- Use approved identity tokens only for country/team identity. They must not signal status, selection, rank, or severity.
- Do not reproduce third-party marks, crests, portraits, or decorative illustration from the generated image.

## Avoid

- Hero imagery or oversized featured-match marketing.
- KPI card walls, uniform nested cards, betting controls, or odds-board cues.
- Decorative orbit lines, fake data visualization, generic gradients, glow, or excessive pills.
- Raw color values in implementation CSS outside the documented identity exception namespace.
