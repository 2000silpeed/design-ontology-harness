# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of Contributor Covenant v2.1 and applies it to design-system work, preset curation, and public issue discussions.

## Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free space for contributors of all backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and identities. Korean-first documentation and English mirrors are both treated as first-class participation paths.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive in reviews.
- Critique code, presets, evidence, and documentation without attacking people.
- Credit public references and avoid copying private design material.
- Be clear when a preset is inspired by a category, not by a single brand clone.
- Accept maintainer decisions around safety, licensing, accessibility, and brand-IP risk.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, sexualized language, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private contact information or private design assets without permission.
- Submitting proprietary Figma files, internal brand systems, customer work, logos, marks, or copyrighted character assets as preset source material.
- Deliberately evading review gates, license notices, deprecation policy, or security checks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or PRs, reject presets, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban contributors when behavior harms the project or community.

Reports can be made through the plugin repository issue tracker. If the report involves private safety or conduct details, avoid posting sensitive evidence publicly; ask a maintainer for a private channel first.

## 한국어 요약

이 프로젝트는 Contributor Covenant v2.1의 취지를 따릅니다. 서로를 존중하고, 코드와 프리셋의 근거를 구체적으로 리뷰하며, 비공개 디자인 자료나 브랜드 자산을 무단으로 사용하지 않습니다. 괴롭힘, 차별적 발언, 위협, 사적 정보 공개, 라이선스 회피는 허용되지 않습니다.

문제가 있으면 플러그인 레포 이슈 트래커로 알려주세요. 민감한 제보는 공개 본문에 세부 증거를 올리지 말고, 먼저 메인테이너에게 비공개 채널을 요청해주세요.
